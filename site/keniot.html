The problem

Your Equipment Gives You Signals. Are You Catching Them?

Machines rarely go from working perfectly to failing without any warning signs. Changes in temperature, vibration, pressure or power consumption can indicate that something isn't operating as expected.

But when sensor data is spread across different systems, identifying these changes and understanding what they mean can be difficult.

KenIoT brings sensor intelligence together to help your team spot unusual patterns and investigate potential problems earlier.

Real-world example

A Machine Doesn't Have to Break Down Before You Notice a Problem.

Imagine a motor running continuously on a factory production line.

For weeks, it operates within its normal temperature and vibration ranges. Then its vibration readings begin changing, even though the machine is still running.

Without continuous monitoring, the change may go unnoticed until the motor overheats, production slows or the equipment fails.

With KenIoT, sensor data can be analysed against the motor's expected operating behaviour. An unusual pattern can trigger an alert, giving the maintenance team a reason to inspect the equipment.

The team can investigate the cause and decide whether maintenance is needed before the problem escalates.

The goal isn't to predict every breakdown perfectly. It's to identify meaningful warning signs earlier and give your team more time to act.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an IoT monitoring platform?

An IoT monitoring platform connects to compatible devices and sensors to collect, display and analyse data. It helps businesses monitor operating conditions, identify unusual readings and understand how connected equipment is performing.

What is KenIoT?

KenIoT is Intelense's IoT and sensor intelligence solution. It brings together data from compatible sensors and industrial systems to support real-time monitoring, anomaly detection and predictive maintenance.

What is predictive maintenance?

Predictive maintenance uses equipment data and condition indicators to identify signs of potential failure. It helps maintenance teams investigate problems and plan servicing based on equipment condition rather than relying only on fixed schedules or waiting for a breakdown.

How does AI anomaly detection work?

AI anomaly detection learns or uses expected operating patterns and compares them with incoming sensor data. When readings deviate from those patterns, the system can flag the change for investigation. Not every anomaly indicates a fault, so alerts may require human review.

What types of sensors can KenIoT monitor?

Depending on supported integrations, KenIoT can work with sensors that measure variables such as temperature, vibration, pressure, humidity, soil moisture and electrical current. The exact range depends on the devices and communication protocols supported by the deployment.

Can KenIoT work with existing industrial equipment?

KenIoT is designed to integrate with compatible existing sensors and industrial systems. For older equipment without digital outputs, additional retrofit sensors or gateways may be required.

How does KenIoT help reduce equipment downtime?

KenIoT can identify unusual sensor patterns that may indicate developing equipment issues. This gives teams an opportunity to investigate and plan maintenance earlier. Actual downtime reduction depends on the equipment, data quality, detection performance and maintenance response.

Does KenIoT automatically create maintenance tickets?

Maintenance tickets or work orders can be created through supported integrations and configured workflows. Availability depends on the maintenance management system and deployment setup.

Can KenIoT monitor multiple sites?

KenIoT is designed to support connected asset monitoring across different operational environments. Multi-site visibility, user permissions and reporting capabilities depend on the deployed configuration.

How long does KenIoT take to identify potential equipment failures?

The time required to identify an anomaly or predict a potential failure varies by equipment type, failure mode, sensor availability and historical data quality. Some abnormal readings may be identified quickly, while reliable failure prediction may require a longer learning period and sufficient operating data.

How can I get started with KenIoT?

Book a demo with Intelense. Share your equipment types, existing sensors, communication protocols and maintenance challenges so the team can help you explore a suitable IoT monitoring setup.
