Capabilities

What KenIoT does.

Six things your team can switch on from day one.

Vibration and temperature

Continuous readings from motors, pumps, compressors and conveyors.

Anomaly detection

Learns each machine's normal pattern and flags drift early.

Failure prediction

Estimates how long until a part needs attention, so you can plan the fix.

Environmental sensing

Air quality, humidity and temperature across rooms and cold chain.

Energy monitoring

Spots machines drawing more power than they should.

Alerts to the right team

Maintenance gets the reading, the trend and the asset location.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Most teams start with a pilot on a few cameras or sensors.

Connect sensors

Bring in the sensors you have, or add new ones where you need them.

Learn normal

KenIoT builds a baseline for each asset over its first weeks of data.

Fix before failure

Drift becomes an alert and a work order, not a breakdown.
