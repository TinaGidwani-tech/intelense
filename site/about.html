  1. 2018

What we believe

How we build.

Three principles shape every module we ship.

Start from what is already there

Most sites already have cameras and sensors. We make them useful instead of asking you to replace them.

Alerts people can trust

An alert nobody believes is noise. We focus on fewer, clearer alerts with the context to act.

Intelligence at the edge

Analysing data close to where it is captured keeps alerts fast and keeps footage on site.
