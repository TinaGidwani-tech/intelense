Capabilities

What KenRobotics does.

Six things your team can switch on from day one.

Obstacle detection

Sees pallets, people and vehicles in the path and reacts in time.

Navigation support

Feeds path and free-space information to the robot's planner.

Autonomous patrol

Runs security rounds and reports anything unusual along the route.

Inspection

Checks gauges, leaks, damage and blocked access on each pass.

Drone analytics

Detection and tracking from the air, on the same engine.

One fleet view

Robot and drone events sit next to your fixed cameras.
