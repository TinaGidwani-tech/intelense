Connect

Plug in existing camera feeds, sensors and audio sources. No rip-and-replace.

Understand

Computer vision and machine learning detect people, vehicles, hazards and unusual activity as it happens.

Respond

Alerts reach the right team with the clip, the location and the context they need to act.

Built for the edge

Analytics close to the camera.

Processing near the source keeps alerts fast and keeps bandwidth low. The same engine powers every Ken module.

At the edge

Video and sensor data is analysed on site, close to your cameras, so alerts arrive in seconds and raw footage does not have to leave the building.

In the Intelense engine

Detection, tracking, voice and sensor models share one engine, so adding a module adds capability, not another system.

With your teams

Alerts, dashboards and reports reach operators, supervisors and responders on the devices they already use.
