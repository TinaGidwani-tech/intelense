FAQ

Common Questions

What does Intelense do?

Intelense builds AI-powered intelligence products that convert video, sensor, and operational data into real-time dashboards, alerts, and business insights.

What is KenVision?

KenVision is the Intelense AI video intelligence product for camera-based analytics, including retail footfall, dwell time, heatmaps, queues, alerts, and operational dashboards.

Can Intelense use existing CCTV cameras?

Yes. Intelense works with your existing cameras and recording systems. No hardware replacement needed just connect and go.

What is retail analytics?

Retail analytics is the process of analyzing in-store behavior such as footfall, dwell time, customer movement, queues, and conversion signals to improve store performance.

What is video analytics for retail?

Video analytics for retail uses AI computer vision to interpret camera footage and convert it into metrics, alerts, and dashboards for store teams.

Does Intelense support edge AI?

Yes. Intelense supports edge and hybrid deployments so video can be processed close to the camera feeds when privacy, latency, or bandwidth matters.

Does Intelense use facial recognition for retail analytics?

Intelense retail analytics focuses on operational behavior metrics, not identifying individual shoppers. Deployments can be configured without facial recognition.

How can I book a demo?

You can book a demo through the Intelense contact page and share your use case, number of sites, camera setup, and priority KPIs.
