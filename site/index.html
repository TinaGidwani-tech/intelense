FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Do we need to replace our cameras?

Intelense is designed to work with the IP cameras and sensors you already run. During a demo we review your current setup and confirm what connects out of the box.

Which module should we start with?

Most teams start with KenVision or KenSafety, because they turn existing camera feeds into alerts fastest. You can add other modules later on the same platform.

What is KenVoice?

KenVoice is our newest module. It adds voice to the Ken platform, so spoken alerts, hands-free commands and call audio sit in the same timeline as your video and sensor events.

How do we get started?

Book a demo. We'll walk through your sites, pick a first use case and set up a pilot on a few cameras so you can see results on your own footage.
