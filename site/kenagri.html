The problem

Your Crops Tell a Story. Are You Seeing the Early Signs?

A healthy-looking field doesn't always tell the whole story.

Water stress, nutrient deficiencies, pests and diseases can develop across different parts of a farm, sometimes before the symptoms become obvious.

Checking every field manually takes time, and decisions based on limited information can lead to unnecessary resource use or missed warning signs.

KenAgri helps you understand what's happening across your fields using AI-powered crop monitoring and agricultural data analytics.

  • Crop problems going unnoticed?Monitor crop conditions and identify areas that may need closer inspection.
  • Not sure when to irrigate?Use available soil moisture, weather and crop information to support irrigation planning.
  • Uncertain about your upcoming harvest?Use yield prediction to help estimate potential production and plan ahead.
  • Pests or diseases spreading across fields?Identify potential problem areas earlier and prioritise field inspections.
  • Managing multiple fields or large estates?Bring available field data into one view to understand conditions across different areas.
Features

Everything You Need to Understand Your Fields Better

KenAgri combines AI crop monitoring, predictive analytics and agricultural data to help you make informed decisions throughout the growing season.

01

AI Crop Health Monitoring

Know which parts of your field need attention.

Analyse available satellite and drone imagery to monitor crop conditions across different field areas. Identify unusual patterns and potential signs of crop stress so you can prioritise inspections and take action where needed.

02

AI Pest & Disease Detection

Spot potential crop problems earlier.

Use AI to analyse available crop imagery and sensor data for patterns associated with potential pests, diseases and crop stress. Identify areas that need closer inspection and help your team investigate problems before they spread further.

03

Crop Yield Prediction

Plan ahead with a clearer picture of your harvest.

Use available crop health information, historical yield records, weather conditions and other field data to estimate potential harvest output.

Yield predictions can help agricultural businesses plan harvesting, labour, storage, transport and market supply more effectively.

04

Smart Irrigation Management

Give your crops water based on what they need.

Use soil moisture readings, weather information and crop requirements to support irrigation planning. Where compatible sensors and irrigation equipment are integrated, KenAgri can support automated irrigation workflows.

For farms without connected irrigation hardware, available insights can help guide manual watering schedules.

05

Soil & Weather Analytics

Understand the conditions shaping your crops.

Bring together available soil, temperature, moisture and weather data to understand how growing conditions change across your fields.

Use these insights to support irrigation planning, crop care and decisions about field management.

06

Satellite & Drone Crop Analysis

Monitor more land without checking every field on foot.

Use satellite imagery and drone-based crop analysis to assess large areas, identify unusual patterns and focus field visits where they are most useful.

Combine aerial observations with ground-level data for a more complete understanding of crop conditions.

How it works

From Field Data to Better Farming Decisions

KenAgri brings available agricultural data together and turns it into insights you can use.

  1. 01

    Connect Your Field Data

    Bring in available satellite imagery, drone images, ground sensor readings, weather information and historical farm records. The data sources used depend on your farm and deployment setup.

  2. 02

    Let AI Analyse Your Crops

    AI analyses the available information to identify crop stress patterns, potential pest and disease risks, changing field conditions and factors that may affect yield.

  3. 03

    Get Insights for Your Next Step

    Review crop health information, yield estimates and irrigation recommendations through available dashboards, reports and alerts. Use these insights to prioritise inspections and plan field operations.

  4. 04

    Monitor and Adjust Throughout the Season

    Continue reviewing field conditions as new imagery and data become available. Use updated insights to support decisions as your crops grow and conditions change.

Industry applications

Precision Agriculture for Different Crops and Growing Environments

Every crop has different needs. KenAgri helps agricultural businesses use available field data to monitor crop conditions and make better-informed decisions across different farming environments.

Real-world example

A Field Can Look Fine from a Distance and Still Need Attention.

Imagine you manage a large farm. Most of your crops appear healthy, but a section of the field is receiving less water than expected.

Without detailed field information, the issue may go unnoticed until the crops begin showing visible signs of stress.

With KenAgri, available satellite or drone imagery and ground sensor data can help identify unusual patterns in that section.

Your team can then inspect the affected area, investigate the cause and decide whether irrigation or another corrective action is needed.

The same approach can help you monitor potential pest and disease concerns, compare crop conditions across fields and plan for the upcoming harvest.

Instead of treating every part of the farm the same way, use field-level insights to focus attention where it matters most.
