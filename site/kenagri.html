Capabilities

What KenAgri does.

Six things your team can switch on from day one.

Crop stress and disease

Flags patches that look different from the rest of the field so you can check them early.

Livestock monitoring

Counts animals and spots unusual behaviour in pens and pastures.

Perimeter and wildlife

Alerts when people or wildlife cross a fence line at night.

Equipment and yard security

Watches machinery, fuel tanks and storage outside working hours.

Drone footage analysis

Runs the same analytics on drone flights over large fields.

Thermal and low light

Works on thermal and infrared cameras after dark.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Most teams start with a pilot on a few cameras or sensors.

Cover the farm

Use existing yard cameras, add field cameras, or upload drone flights.

Set what matters

Choose fields, pens and fence lines to watch, and what counts as unusual.

Act early

Get alerts with location and a snapshot, and track issues across the season.
