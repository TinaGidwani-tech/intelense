How it works

From Your Existing Cameras to Smarter Home Monitoring

KenHome adds AI-powered monitoring to compatible cameras, helping you identify selected events without having to watch every screen yourself.

  1. 01

    Connect Your Existing Cameras

    Integrate compatible indoor, outdoor, doorbell or driveway cameras with KenHome. Reuse your current setup where supported, subject to camera compatibility and deployment requirements.

  2. 02

    Set Up Your Monitoring Zones

    Choose the areas you want to monitor, such as entrances, living spaces, driveways or designated restricted zones. Configure the events and alerts relevant to your household.

  3. 03

    Let AI Monitor for Selected Events

    KenHome analyses supported camera feeds to identify configured events, such as potential falls, unusual inactivity, restricted-zone entry and suspicious activity.

  4. 04

    Receive Alerts When Something Needs Attention

    When a configured event is detected, KenHome can notify designated family members or caregivers through supported channels. Review the available event information and decide what action is needed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is KenHome?

KenHome is Intelense's AI-powered home security and monitoring solution. It uses compatible camera feeds to help identify configured security and safety events involving property access, elderly monitoring, children, pets and vehicles.

How is KenHome different from a regular home security camera?

A traditional security camera primarily records footage and may provide basic motion notifications. KenHome adds AI-powered event analysis to help identify selected activities and provide more context about what may need attention.

Can KenHome work with my existing security cameras?

KenHome is designed to work with compatible existing cameras, including supported indoor, outdoor and driveway setups. The exact compatibility depends on the camera model, video stream and deployment configuration.

Can KenHome detect falls in elderly people?

KenHome supports camera-based fall monitoring in compatible setups. Detection depends on camera placement, visibility and configuration. Alerts should supplement regular care arrangements and must not be treated as a replacement for emergency response or medical monitoring.

Can KenHome help keep children safe at home?

KenHome can monitor configured zones and flag supported events, such as entry into a designated restricted area. It provides an additional layer of awareness, not a guarantee that every unsafe situation will be detected.

Can I use KenHome to monitor my pets?

Yes, supported pet-monitoring features can help you review activity in camera-covered areas and receive configured alerts for selected events or restricted-zone entry.

Can KenHome monitor my driveway and parked car?

KenHome supports driveway and vehicle monitoring through compatible cameras and configured detection capabilities. Available alerts depend on the setup and events the system is configured to recognise.

How quickly does KenHome send alerts?

Alert speed depends on the event type, deployment configuration, network connectivity and notification channel. Contact Intelense for the expected performance of your proposed setup.

Does KenHome use facial recognition?

KenHome's published configuration describes zone-based rules and behaviour patterns rather than facial recognition for distinguishing expected activity from potential threats. Confirm the exact configuration and privacy settings with Intelense before deployment.

Can multiple family members receive alerts?

KenHome supports configurable family access according to the published product information. Available permissions, notification channels and access controls depend on the deployed configuration.

Does KenHome replace a professional security system or caregiver?

No. KenHome is intended to support home security and family awareness. It does not replace professional security arrangements, direct supervision, caregiving or emergency services.

How much does KenHome cost?

Pricing depends on factors such as the number of cameras, monitored zones, required features and deployment configuration. Contact Intelense for a quote based on your home's needs.

How can I get started with KenHome?

Book a demo with Intelense. Share your home layout, existing camera setup and monitoring priorities so the team can help you understand the suitable configuration.
