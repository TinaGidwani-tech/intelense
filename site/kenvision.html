Capabilities

What KenVision does.

Six things your team can switch on from day one.

Intrusion detection

Draw a zone on any camera and get an alert the moment someone enters it out of hours.

Crowd density

Count people per zone and flag crowding before it becomes unsafe.

Vehicle tracking

Follow vehicles across cameras and flag stops in fire lanes, loading bays or no-go areas.

Loitering and dwell time

Spot people or vehicles that stay too long in sensitive places.

Objects left behind

Flag bags, boxes and equipment left unattended in public spaces.

Video search

Search recorded footage by person, vehicle, colour or time instead of scrubbing through hours.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Most teams start with a pilot on a few cameras or sensors.

Connect

Point KenVision at the IP cameras you already run. No new cameras needed for most sites.

Configure

Draw zones, choose what to detect and set who gets each alert.

Act

Operators get a clip, a location and a confidence score, then acknowledge, escalate or dismiss.
