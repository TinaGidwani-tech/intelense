The problem

Safety Shouldn't Depend on Someone Watching Every Screen.

On a busy construction site, factory floor or warehouse, things can change in seconds.

A worker may enter a restricted area, operate without the required protective equipment or move too close to heavy machinery.

Traditional CCTV records what happens. But reviewing every camera feed continuously is difficult, especially across large sites.

KenSafety uses AI to help identify potential safety risks as they appear on camera.

  • Missing protective equipment?Identify potential PPE violations, such as missing helmets or high-visibility vests.
  • Workers entering dangerous areas?Monitor restricted zones and flag unauthorised entry.
  • People and machinery working too close?Detect configured human-vehicle safety-zone violations.
  • Fire, smoke or a potential fall?Flag supported visual events so the relevant team can respond.
  • Too much paperwork after an incident?Maintain incident records and reports to support safety reviews and compliance documentation.

AI PPE Detection & Compliance

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI workplace safety monitoring?

AI workplace safety monitoring uses artificial intelligence and computer vision to analyse camera feeds for safety-related events, such as missing protective equipment, restricted-zone entry and selected hazards. It helps safety teams identify potential issues without relying entirely on manual camera monitoring.

What is KenSafety?

KenSafety is Intelense's AI-powered safety monitoring solution. It uses compatible camera feeds to help detect PPE violations, restricted-zone entry, human-vehicle safety risks, fire, smoke and supported incidents, with alerts and reporting capabilities.

What is AI PPE detection?

AI PPE detection uses computer vision to identify whether workers appear to be wearing required personal protective equipment, such as helmets, high-visibility vests, gloves and boots, within camera-visible areas.

Can KenSafety work with existing CCTV cameras?

Yes, KenSafety is designed to integrate with compatible existing CCTV cameras and recording systems. The exact compatibility depends on the camera setup, video streams and deployment requirements.

Can KenSafety detect restricted-area entry?

KenSafety can monitor configured restricted zones and flag unauthorised entry when the relevant capability is enabled. This helps teams identify potential access and safety violations.

Can KenSafety detect fire, smoke and falls?

KenSafety supports configured fire, smoke and fall-related detection capabilities. Performance depends on camera positioning, visibility, environmental conditions and deployment configuration. Alerts should complement established emergency procedures and safety systems.

Which industries can use KenSafety?

KenSafety is suited to use cases in construction, manufacturing, warehouses, logistics, ports, infrastructure and other environments where monitoring safety conditions through cameras is useful.

Does KenSafety replace safety officers?

No. KenSafety is designed to support safety teams, not replace them. AI detection, alerts and reports help teams identify potential issues, but people remain responsible for assessment, intervention and safety decisions.

Does KenSafety provide compliance reports?

KenSafety supports incident records and safety reporting to help teams review events and monitor compliance trends. The specific reports available depend on the deployment.

How can I get started with KenSafety?

Book a demo with Intelense. Share your industry, main safety concerns and existing camera setup so the team can explain how KenSafety may fit your requirements.
