Capabilities

What KenSafety does.

Six things your team can switch on from day one.

PPE compliance

Checks for hard hats and hi-vis vests where your rules require them.

Restricted and hazard zones

Alerts when people enter areas around machinery, docks or chemicals.

Slips, trips and falls

Detects a person on the ground and raises an alert straight away.

Forklift and pedestrian proximity

Flags close calls between vehicles and people on shared floors.

Blocked exits and walkways

Spots pallets and equipment left in emergency routes.

Safety trends

Weekly reports show where and when risks cluster, so you can fix the cause.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Most teams start with a pilot on a few cameras or sensors.

Map your rules

Mark PPE zones, hazard areas and walkways on your existing camera views.

Watch every shift

KenSafety checks every frame against those rules, around the clock.

Coach, not just catch

Supervisors get the clip and context, and trends show where training helps most.
