## KenSafety — Construction Site Safety

**Challenge:** High incident rate on active construction site with 200+ workers across multiple zones.

**Solution:** KenSafety deployed across existing site cameras — PPE compliance monitoring, hazard zone enforcement, fire detection.

**Results:**
- 80% fewer on-site safety incidents
- 95% PPE compliance rate achieved within 2 weeks
- Automated daily safety reports replacing manual audits
- 48-hour deployment, zero downtime

## KenVision — Retail Store Performance

**Challenge:** National retailer unable to measure store conversion or optimise floor layout without expensive manual audits.

**Solution:** KenVision connected to existing CCTV — footfall counting, heatmaps, queue detection, conversion tracking across 12 locations.

**Results:**
- +28% conversion uplift within 60 days
- Peak-hour staffing optimised, reducing labour cost by 18%
- Queue alerts reduced average wait time by 35%
- Zero cameras replaced

## KenHome — Smart Home Safety

**Challenge:** Family with elderly parent and young children requiring 24/7 monitoring without intrusive hardware.

**Solution:** KenHome deployed on existing home cameras — fall detection, child safety monitoring, intruder alerts, vehicle protection.

**Results:**
- 45% reduction in energy waste via occupancy-based automation
- 3 fall events detected and alerted within 4 seconds
- Zero false alarm activations in 90-day period
- Live in under 24 hours
