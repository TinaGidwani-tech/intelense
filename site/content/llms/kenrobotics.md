## What KenRobotics Does

KenRobotics provides the AI perception and coordination layer for autonomous robots and unmanned systems — enabling real-time environment awareness, safe navigation, and large-scale fleet orchestration.

- **Real-Time Perception** — Sub-3-second environment mapping and obstacle detection
- **Dynamic Path Planning** — Continuously recalculated routes based on live environment data
- **Multi-Robot Coordination** — Orchestrate fleets of robots with collision avoidance and task assignment
- **Fleet Management** — Monitor robot health, battery, task progress, and deployment status
- **Warehouse Automation** — Picking, packing, sorting, and inventory management support
- **Autonomous Navigation** — Indoor and outdoor navigation without fixed infrastructure
