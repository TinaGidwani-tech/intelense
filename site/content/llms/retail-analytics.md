## What It Does

Intelense turns existing retail CCTV into a full analytics platform — no new cameras, no disruption. Retailers get real-time footfall, shopper behaviour, and conversion data from day one.

## Capabilities

- **Footfall Analytics** — Count shoppers entering and moving through the store in real time
- **Heatmap Analytics** — Visualise the hottest and coldest zones across the store floor
- **Queue Analytics** — Monitor queue length and wait times, trigger staff alerts at thresholds
- **CCTV Analytics** — Extract intelligence from any existing camera brand or resolution
- **Video Analytics** — Behavioural analysis including dwell time and path tracking
- **Conversion Tracking** — Correlate footfall with POS data to measure conversion rate

- 95% footfall counting accuracy

- <3 second alert latency
- 0 cameras replaced — works with existing infrastructure

- Staff scheduling aligned to peak footfall hours
