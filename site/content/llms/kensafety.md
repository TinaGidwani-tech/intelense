## What KenSafety Does

KenSafety monitors construction sites, factories, and industrial facilities in real time — detecting unsafe behaviours, enforcing PPE compliance, and triggering alerts before incidents escalate.

## Core Capabilities

- **PPE Compliance** — Detects helmets, vests, gloves, goggles in real time
- **Hazard Zone Detection** — Alerts when workers enter restricted or dangerous areas
- **Fire & Smoke AI** — Camera-based fire and smoke detection with instant alerts
- **Fall Detection** — Detects worker falls and triggers immediate notification to safety staff
- **Trespass Detection** — Alerts on unauthorised access to protected zones
- **Automated Incident Reports** — AI-generated safety reports with timestamped evidence
- **Safety Dashboard** — Live overview of compliance across all sites and zones
