## What KenAgri Does

KenAgri fuses data from satellites, drones, IoT sensors, and ground cameras into a unified crop intelligence layer — delivering field-level prescriptions for irrigation, pest control, and harvest timing at scale.

## Core Capabilities

- **Crop Health Monitoring** — Detect stress, disease, and nutrient deficiency across fields
- **Yield Prediction** — AI-driven forecasts based on 50+ environmental and crop data points
- **Pest & Disease Detection** — Early identification of infestations before they spread
- **Automated Irrigation** — Trigger irrigation based on soil moisture, weather, and crop stage
- **Weather Correlation** — Adjust recommendations dynamically against live weather data
- **Input Optimisation** — Reduce fertiliser and pesticide waste with precision application maps
