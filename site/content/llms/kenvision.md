## What KenVision Does

KenVision transforms any existing camera network into a real-time behavioral intelligence layer. It counts people, maps movement patterns, detects queues, and tracks conversion — all from cameras already on-site.

## Core Capabilities

- **Footfall Counting** — Accurate people counting across entry/exit zones and store sections
- **Zone Heatmaps** — Visual maps of where customers spend the most time
- **Dwell Time Analysis** — Measure how long visitors engage with displays or zones
- **Queue Detection** — Real-time queue length monitoring and overflow alerts
- **Conversion Tracking** — Correlate footfall with sales to measure conversion rates
- **Staff Optimisation** — Align staffing levels to live traffic patterns
