# FAQ — Intelense AI Products, Deployment & Pricing

> Common questions about Intelense AI products, deployment requirements, hardware, integration, edge AI, and privacy.

**URL:** https://www.intelense.com/faq

## What does Intelense do?

Intelense builds AI video analytics products that connect to existing camera networks to deliver real-time intelligence — footfall analytics, workplace safety monitoring, home security, crop health, IoT sensor analytics, and autonomous robotics coordination.

## What is KenVision?

KenVision is Intelense's AI video intelligence module. It counts footfall, maps customer movement with heatmaps, detects queues, measures dwell time, and tracks conversion rate — all from existing CCTV cameras.

## Do I need to replace my cameras?

No. All Intelense products work with existing cameras regardless of brand or resolution. No hardware replacement is required.

## What is retail analytics?

Retail analytics is the process of collecting and analysing shopper behaviour data inside a store. Intelense delivers this through AI applied to existing CCTV footage — providing footfall counts, heatmaps, queue times, and conversion rates in real time.

## Does Intelense support edge AI?

Yes. All processing happens at the edge — on the device or local server — for real-time speed and data privacy. No video is sent to external servers.

## Does Intelense use facial recognition?

No. Intelense does not use facial recognition or store any biometric data. People are tracked as anonymous movement paths only.

## How long does deployment take?

Most deployments go live in under 48 hours. No hardware replacement, no construction, no long IT projects.

## What cameras are supported?

Intelense works with any IP camera or CCTV system. Brand and resolution agnostic.

Pricing is available on request and is based on the number of cameras, modules selected, and deployment scale. Contact sales for a custom quote.
