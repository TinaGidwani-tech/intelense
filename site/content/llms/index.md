# Intelense — AI Video Analytics Platform

> Intelligence at the Edge. Six AI modules for retail, safety, home, agriculture, IoT, and robotics — powered by a single unified engine.

**URL:** https://www.intelense.com/

## What Intelense Does

Intelense is an AI video analytics platform that turns any existing camera network into an intelligent sensing layer. It delivers real-time insights across six domains through modular AI products that deploy in under 48 hours, require no hardware replacement, and run at the edge for privacy and speed.

## Six AI Modules

| Module | Domain | Key Capability |
|---|---|---|
| KenVision | Retail & Spaces | Footfall, heatmaps, queues, conversion |
| KenSafety | Workplace Safety | PPE compliance, hazard detection, incident alerts |
| KenHome | Home Security | Intruder, elderly/child monitoring, vehicle protection |
| KenAgri | Agriculture | Crop health, yield prediction, pest detection |
| KenIoT | IoT / Industrial | Predictive maintenance, sensor anomaly detection |
| KenRobotics | Autonomous Systems | Robot perception, navigation, fleet coordination |

All six modules are unified under the Ken360 platform — one API, one dashboard, one data model.
