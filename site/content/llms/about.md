# About Intelense

> Intelense builds AI products for the real world. Founded 2018 by Dinesh Prasanna (MIT TLP). Six AI modules, one unified platform.

**URL:** https://www.intelense.com/about

## Company

Intelense was founded in 2018 by Dinesh Prasanna, a graduate of the MIT Technology Leadership Program (TLP). The company builds edge AI video analytics products that work with existing infrastructure — no new hardware, no lengthy deployments.

## Core Values

- **Accuracy First** — 99% accuracy across all modules. No guesswork, no false positives that waste time.
- **Speed at Scale** — <3 second latency from camera to alert, across thousands of endpoints simultaneously.
- **Privacy by Design** — All processing happens at the edge. No faces stored, no data sent to third parties.
