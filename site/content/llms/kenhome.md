## What KenHome Does

KenHome provides 24/7 AI-powered monitoring for residential properties — detecting threats, monitoring vulnerable occupants, and protecting vehicles using existing home cameras.

## Core Capabilities

- **Intruder Detection** — Detect loitering, trespassing, and break-in attempts with real-time alerts
- **Elderly Monitoring** — Fall detection and unusual inactivity alerts for elderly occupants
- **Child Safety** — Monitor children's activity and detect hazardous situations
- **Pet Monitoring** — Track pet activity and detect potential hazards
- **Vehicle Protection** — Detect car theft attempts, tampering, and suspicious proximity
- **Real-Time Alerts** — Push notifications to phone within 5 seconds of detection
