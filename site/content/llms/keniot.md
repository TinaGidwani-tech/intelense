# KenIoT — IoT & Sensor Analytics AI

> AI-powered IoT sensor analytics. Predictive failure detection, real-time anomaly alerts, automated maintenance scheduling.

**URL:** https://www.intelense.com/keniot

## What KenIoT Does

KenIoT ingests data from industrial IoT sensors and learns baseline operational patterns — detecting anomalies, predicting equipment failures before they occur, and scheduling maintenance automatically.

## Core Capabilities

- **Predictive Failure Detection** — Identify equipment degradation patterns before breakdown
- **Real-Time Anomaly Alerts** — Instant notification when sensor readings deviate from baseline
- **Baseline Model Learning** — AI continuously learns normal operating conditions per device
- **Environmental Variable Accounting** — Adjusts thresholds based on temperature, humidity, load
- **Automated Maintenance Scheduling** — Trigger work orders based on predicted failure windows
- **Multi-Sensor Fusion** — Combine vibration, temperature, pressure, current, and flow data

## Industries

Railways, Defence, Agriculture, Healthcare, Fleet Management, Manufacturing, Energy.

## Key Benefits

- Reduce unplanned downtime
- Extend equipment lifespan
- Optimise maintenance schedules
- Lower operational costs
