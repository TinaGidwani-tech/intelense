Section 01

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you contact us, request a demo, or create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, company name, phone number, and the content of your communications with us.

Product Analytics Data

Our AI products process visual and sensor data within your infrastructure. For camera-based products, all video processing occurs at the edge raw video footage is never transmitted to or stored on Intelense servers. Only anonymised analytics outputs (counts, aggregated metrics, event classifications) are retained.

Usage and Technical Data

We collect information about how you use our platform, including log files, IP addresses, browser type, device identifiers, and session data. This data helps us improve our products and diagnose technical issues.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our AI products and services
  • To respond to enquiries, support requests, and feedback
  • To send product updates, security notices, and marketing communications (with your consent)
  • To improve our AI models and product performance using anonymised, aggregated data
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
Section 03

Data Processing and Privacy by Design

Intelense's products are built on a privacy-by-design architecture:

  • Edge processing: All video analysis is performed locally. Raw footage never leaves your network.
  • Anonymisation: Individuals captured on camera are not identified or tracked across sessions. Analytics are aggregated and non-identifiable by default.
  • Data minimisation: We only collect the data necessary to provide our services.
  • Retention limits: Anonymised analytics data is retained for the period specified in your service agreement, then deleted.
Section 04

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who assist us in delivering our products (cloud hosting, analytics, support tooling) under strict data processing agreements
  • Professional advisors including lawyers, auditors, and insurers as necessary
  • Regulators and law enforcement when required by law or to protect our rights
  • Successors in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets
Section 05

International Data Transfers

Intelense operates from Canada and India. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission for EU/EEA data subjects.

Section 06

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to data portability and to withdraw consent for marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@intelense.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

Section 07

Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to ensure functionality, and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You may opt out of analytics cookies through our cookie preference centre or your browser settings. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Section 08

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit, encryption at rest, access controls, and regular penetration testing. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a prominent notice on our website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

Section 11

Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact our Privacy Officer:

  • Email: privacy@intelense.ca
  • Mail: Intelense Inc., Privacy Officer, Toronto, Ontario, Canada
