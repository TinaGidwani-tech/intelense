Capabilities

What KenVoice does.

Six things your team can switch on from day one.

Spoken alerts

Plays clear voice alerts on site speakers and radios when an event fires.

Hands-free commands

Workers and operators can query and acknowledge alerts by voice.

Call and radio transcription

Turns calls and radio traffic into searchable text.

Keyword detection

Listens for words like "help" or "fire" and raises an alert.

One incident timeline

Voice, video and sensor events land in the same place.

Works with your devices

Built to connect to speakers, radios and phone lines.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Most teams start with a pilot on a few cameras or sensors.

Connect audio

Link speakers, radios, phone lines or microphones.

Choose the words

Set the keywords, commands and alerts that matter on each site.

Close the loop

Every spoken alert and response is logged with the incident.
