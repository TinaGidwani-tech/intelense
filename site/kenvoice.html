What KenVoice does

Understand More Than What Your Customers Buy

Sales reports show what customers purchased. They don't always show what happened during the conversation before a sale — or why a customer walked away.

KenVoice analyses in-store conversations to help retailers understand how staff greet customers, recommend products, handle questions, and identify opportunities to upsell or cross-sell.

With conversation insights and staff scorecards, managers can make coaching more specific and identify areas for improvement.

How it works

From Conversation to Actionable Insights

  1. 01

    Set Up at the Counter

    Place a compatible tablet at the point of sale. KenVoice is designed for in-person retail conversations, without requiring headsets or phone-system integration.

  2. 02

    AI Analyses the Conversation

    On-device AI processes speech, separates staff and customer voices, and converts the conversation into text and structured insights.

  3. 03

    Review Insights on Your Dashboard

    Managers can review conversation tags, scores, staff performance, and potential missed sales opportunities through dashboards and reports.

  4. 04

    Coach with Context

    Use conversation transcripts and interaction scores to give staff specific feedback, recognise effective selling behaviours, and improve customer service.

Privacy

Conversation Intelligence with Privacy in Mind

KenVoice is designed to process audio on the device rather than upload raw recordings to the cloud.

  • Audio is processed on the tablet and discarded after processing.
  • Text transcripts and structured insights can be synced to the cloud.
  • Staff voiceprints are encrypted and treated as biometric data.
  • Access is controlled through role-based permissions and audit logs.
  • Transcript retention can be configured.

Retailers should establish appropriate staff and customer notices, consent processes, access policies, and retention settings for their deployment and applicable local requirements.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is KenVoice?

KenVoice is an AI audio analytics platform for physical retail stores. It analyses customer and staff conversations to identify service patterns, sales opportunities, and coaching insights.

How is KenVoice different from call-centre analytics?

Call-centre tools typically analyse phone calls or digital meetings. KenVoice is designed for face-to-face conversations at retail counters, using a tablet-based setup and retail-specific analytics.

Does KenVoice record and store customer audio?

KenVoice processes audio on the device and discards it after processing. Text transcripts and insights may be synced to the cloud according to the configured system and retention settings.

Do we need to change our POS or phone system?

KenVoice is designed to operate from a tablet placed at the counter, without requiring phone-system integration. Confirm any deployment-specific requirements with the Intelense team.

How does KenVoice identify staff and customer speech?

Its speech-processing workflow separates staff and customer voices to help structure conversations and generate interaction insights. Identification and configuration depend on the deployment.

Can KenVoice help improve staff performance?

KenVoice provides conversation tags, scores, transcripts, and staff scorecards that managers can use to identify coaching opportunities and review service patterns.

Can KenVoice be used across multiple stores?

The platform describes dashboards for retail teams and management. Ask the Intelense team about the multi-store setup, permissions, and reporting options for your requirements.
